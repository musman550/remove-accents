Remove Accents: What it does

This tool removes accents and other marks from Latin letters, converting café to cafe and señor to senor. It also handles special characters like ß, æ, ø, and ł. The tool preserves the original capitalization of letters and updates in real-time as you type. It operates entirely within your browser, ensuring that no text is uploaded or stored. While it works well for making file names or slugs, searching text without accents, cleaning name lists, or preparing text for systems that accept only plain letters, it's important to note that removing accents can change the meaning of words in certain languages.
